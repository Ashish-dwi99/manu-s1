    Is this cheque-bounce complaint on time?

    About 35 lakh cheque-bounce cases are pending. Each complaint has three deadlines the magistrate must check: was the cheque presented in time, was the notice sent in time, and was the complaint filed after the payment period ended but within one month. The court calculator does the date arithmetic exactly; the model reads the complaint and decides. These four complaints are samples written for this demo.

    Runs inside the court

    One 4-billion-parameter model on one GPU in the court's own server room. Case files never leave the building, as the Supreme Court's draft AI rules and the DPDP Act expect.

    Says when it can't tell

    Every answer carries its confidence. When the record is silent, the model says so. The court sets a threshold; anything below it goes to a person.

    Procedure, never merits

    It checks dates, filings, appearances and records. It never scores a person, predicts an outcome or suggests bail: those are the judge's alone.
